## Research

**LLM diagnosis: benchmarks vs real inputs.** Strong scores on curated cases sit beside preprints showing text overriding images, overconfidence, and multi-agent gains that reverse on real emergency notes. ([1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42912144/), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.24.26363897v3), [3](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.17.26365809v1), [4](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.17.26365807v1), [5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31586v1), [6](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42893001/))

**Measuring human-AI oversight.** Studies move from accuracy to interaction: readers anchoring on AI, errors reviewers miss in AI drafts, directional disagreement metrics, and faster inbox handling. ([1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.20.26365977v1), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.16.26365578v1), [3](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.15.26365116v1), [4](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.19.26364791v1), [5](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.19.26365557v1), [6](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42925890/))

**Audits exposing hidden failures.** Skin-tone-dependent labels, skull implants that cannot be implanted, weak OCT grounding, per-patient privacy risk, and digital twins whose accuracy does not track treatment ranking. ([1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30901v1), [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30035v1), [3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31205v1), [4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30229v1), [5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30577v1), [6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29830v1))

- **[Multi-agent LLM diagnostic teams improve differential recall on curated cases and reverse on real emergency notes: a label-level reversal without a clinician-judged safety difference](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.24.26363897v3)** — Preprint: multi-agent LLM teams improve recall on curated vignettes but reverse on 364 real emergency notes. _(medRxiv)_
- **[Clinical referral text overrides MRI evidence in multimodal large language models: glioma versus brain abscess](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.17.26365809v1)** — Preprint: referral wording swung four multimodal LLMs' glioma-vs-abscess calls far more than the MRI did. _(medRxiv)_
- **[From clinician review to measurable human oversight of AI-drafted paediatric discharge documents](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.20.26365977v1)** — Randomized preprint measures what clinicians catch in LLM discharge drafts; most wrong abbreviation expansions got through. _(medRxiv)_

## Gaps in this week's research

1. **Diagnostic LLMs not tested on live, messy clinical inputs** Benchmark used curated CPC and MIMIC cases. Preprints: multi-agent gains reversed on real ED notes, and referral text overrode MRI findings. ([1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42912144/), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.24.26363897v3), [3](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.17.26365809v1)) _Study:_ Prospective silent-mode study on live ED notes with adjudicated final diagnoses, including arms with misleading referral text.
2. **Unknown which AI error types clinician review actually catches** Reviewers removed 18/22 unsupported additions but only 6/22 wrong abbreviations. Autoantibody readers' revised ratings almost all moved toward the model. ([1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.20.26365977v1), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.16.26365578v1), [3](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.15.26365116v1)) _Study:_ Seeded-error audits of AI drafting and second-read tools in use, reporting catch rate and anchoring for each error type.
3. **Patient-facing health AI lacks real-user testing outside English** SIM-UT tested chatbots in India, South Africa and Kenya with simulated users only. Drug-safety warnings weaker in Russian, Arabic and Hebrew Wikipedia. ([1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30412v1), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.20.26366071v1), [3](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.20.26365983v1), [4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31256v1)) _Study:_ Prospective multilingual audit of live triage-chatbot conversations, with clinician-rated safety errors by language and literacy.
